# Library Borrowing and Access Policy

Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B. It is not an official FAST-NUCES policy.

## 1. Borrowing limits

1.1 An undergraduate student may borrow up to five books at one time.

1.2 The loan period for a book is fourteen days. Reference books and theses may not leave the library.

1.3 A loan may be renewed once, provided no other reader has reserved the item.

## 2. Fines and access

2.1 A late return is charged a fine of Rs. 20 per book for every day the book is overdue.

2.2 The main library is open from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday, during the semester.
