# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2027 edition)

This edition replaces the 2026 edition for students beginning in Fall 2027.

## 1. Attendance

1.1 A student must attend at least 80 percent of lectures to sit the final examination. Documented medical absences may be considered by the department.

## 2. Academic warning

2.1 A student whose cumulative grade point average falls below 2.00 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic warning.

## 3. Repeating a course

3.1 A student may repeat any course to improve the cumulative grade point average. The most recent attempt determines the recorded grade.
