# Need-Based Scholarship Policy

Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B. It is not an official FAST-NUCES policy.

## 1. Application

1.1 A student applies for a need-based scholarship through the Financial Aid Office within the first four weeks of a semester.

1.2 The application includes household income evidence and the latest academic transcript.

## 2. Award and renewal

2.1 The scholarship review committee meets once each semester and may award up to 75 percent of the tuition fee.

2.2 A scholarship is renewed only if the student keeps a CGPA of at least 2.50 and a fresh income statement is submitted.
