# Faculty Entrepreneurship and Technical Start-ups Policy (Revision, November 2026)

Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B. It stands in for a newer version of the May 2022 policy in the corpus and is not an official FAST-NUCES policy.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This revision replaces the May 2022 policy for all full-time faculty members from 1 December 2026.

## 2. Equity and time

2.1 A faculty member may hold an equity stake of up to 20 percent in a technical start-up that licenses university intellectual property.

2.2 A faculty member may spend at most one working day per week on start-up activities, with written approval of the Dean.
