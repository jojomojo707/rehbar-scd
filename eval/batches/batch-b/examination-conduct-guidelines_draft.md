# Examination Conduct Guidelines

Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B (draft circulated for comments). It is not an official FAST-NUCES policy.

## 1. Before the examination

1.1 Students must carry a valid university identity card into every examination hall.

1.2 A student who arrives more than 30 minutes after the start of a final examination is not allowed to sit the paper.

## 2. During the examination

2.1 Mobile phones, smart watches and notes must be left outside the examination hall.

2.2 A student found using unfair means receives an F grade in the course and the case is referred to the Disciplinary Committee.
