# Academic Accommodation and Accessibility Policy

Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B. It is not an official FAST-NUCES policy.

## 1. Requests

1.1 A student who needs an academic accommodation contacts the Student Support Office before the semester starts, with supporting medical or psychological documentation.

1.2 Approved accommodations can include extra examination time, a separate examination room and accessible seating.

## 2. Confidentiality

2.1 Only the instructors who must arrange an approved accommodation receive the accommodation plan; the underlying diagnosis is never shared with them.
