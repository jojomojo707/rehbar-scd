Synthetic test document for Assignment 03 Batch B. Its front matter deliberately has no title and no date or version.

## 1. Registering a society

1.1 A new society needs at least fifteen student members and a faculty advisor before the Student Affairs Office registers it.

1.2 Each registered society submits an annual activity report at the end of the spring semester.
